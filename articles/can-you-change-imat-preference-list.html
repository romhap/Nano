Can You Change Your University Preference List After You've Already Submitted It?

Maya Sep 18, 2026 · 4 min read

A lot of candidates submit their university preference list on Universitaly, then keep second-guessing it for weeks afterward, assuming it's locked the second they click submit. It usually isn't. Whether you can still touch it, and how much, depends entirely on where you are in the registration timeline, and getting that wrong in either direction causes real problems.

Why this question comes up so often

The preference list gets submitted early, often before most candidates have finished studying, let alone finalized how they actually feel about each university on it. It's normal for that list to look different a month later once you've researched more, talked to current students, or just changed your mind about a city. The anxiety isn't irrational, it's just aimed at the wrong moment in the process.

What you can usually still edit

During the open registration window, Universitaly typically lets you log back in and revise your list, reorder it, add universities, or remove them, as many times as you want. Nothing is final just because you submitted once. Treat the first submission as a working draft you're allowed to improve, not a binding decision, right up until that window actually closes.

What locks in once the window closes

Once registration closes for that step, the list you have on file is the one that gets used when results come out and the placement system runs through the national ranking. There's no editing it after that point to react to your score or to anything you've learned since. This is exactly why the list deserves real thought before the deadline, not a rushed version you meant to fix later.

The mistake this actually causes

The real risk isn't editing too much, it's treating the early submission as final and never coming back to revise it at all. Candidates lock in a short, rushed first draft, then run out of window to fix it once they've done more research. Always confirm the exact dates for your specific registration cycle directly on Universitaly, since they shift from year to year, but the safe habit is the same every year: submit early, then keep revisiting it until the door actually closes.
