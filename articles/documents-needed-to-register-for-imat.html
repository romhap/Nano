The Documents You Actually Need Before You Can Register for the IMAT

Maya Sep 8, 2026 · 4 min read

Registration feels like the boring administrative step standing between you and the actual studying. Most candidates treat it that way right up until they open the registration portal a week before the deadline and realize they're missing something they needed weeks to sort out. None of it is complicated. All of it needs more lead time than people expect.

The identity documents nobody double-checks in time

You need a valid passport or national ID, and "valid" means valid well past the test date, not just currently not expired. You'll also need a recent passport-style photo that meets specific format requirements, which sounds trivial until your first upload gets rejected for the wrong background or wrong dimensions.

The academic documents you need lined up early

Your high school diploma or a statement confirming you're on track to receive one, plus your transcript, needs to be ready in the format the system asks for. If your school issues documents in a language other than English or Italian, factor in the time an official translation actually takes. Schools are not always fast about this, and it is not something you can rush the week before you need it.

The account itself trips people up more than the documents do

Setting up your Universitaly account and completing your application is its own process with its own deadline, and that deadline is not the same date as the test itself. People who treat registration as a formality they'll handle "sometime before the test" often discover the actual registration window closed earlier than they assumed.

The easy thing to miss

Keep proof of any required payment. A confirmation email is not always enough if a question comes up later, so save the actual receipt or transaction record somewhere you can find it, not buried in an inbox you'll forget to search.

Once the paperwork is actually sorted, the real question becomes whether you're using your remaining prep time well. That's what 1:1 mentorship is for, not the registration process itself, but making sure the weeks you have left are spent on the right things.

Apply for the mentorship

Nobody fails the IMAT because of a missing document. Plenty of people stress themselves out unnecessarily over one. Sort this part early, and it stops being something you have to think about at all.
