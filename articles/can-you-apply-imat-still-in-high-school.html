Can You Apply to the IMAT While Still in High School?

Rom Jun 30, 2026 · 4 min read

This question comes up constantly from candidates in their final year of school, usually with some anxiety attached, as if applying too early might disqualify them somehow. It won't, but the timing does matter in specific ways.

Registering before you graduate is normal, not risky

Plenty of candidates register for and sit the IMAT during their final year of secondary school, before their diploma is formally issued. The exam itself doesn't require a completed diploma in hand on test day - what matters is that you'll have it, or an equivalent, by the time enrollment actually happens.

Where the diploma actually matters

Your finished diploma and final transcript become relevant at the enrollment stage, after you've been offered a seat, not before. If you're accepted with results still pending or a diploma still being finalized, universities generally expect it to follow shortly after, within a defined window, rather than demanding it upfront at registration.

What changes for non-EU candidates specifically

Because the non-EU track involves earlier consulate-based steps, the practical timing can be tighter, and it's worth confirming with your specific consulate how they handle an in-progress diploma rather than assuming it works the same way everywhere. This is exactly the kind of country-specific detail that varies enough to be worth checking directly rather than trusting a general answer.

What we'd actually suggest

Don't wait for your final results to start engaging with the process. Register on the expected timeline, prepare for the exam on your normal schedule, and treat the diploma paperwork as a parallel track that needs to be sorted by enrollment, not a prerequisite for sitting the test itself.
